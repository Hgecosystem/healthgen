What we do

Capability that goes wherever the problem takes it.

Health Generation works across eight connected capability areas. Most engagements start with one — and pull in others once the full picture becomes clear.

Quality & Clinical Governance Care Planning Support Remediation & Transformation Workforce & Capability Funding & Sustainability Assurance & Readiness Care Intelligence & Technology Commissioning & New Services

01 — Quality & Clinical Governance

Understand risk. Strengthen the systems behind care.

When something feels wrong — a complaint, an incident, a regulatory signal — providers need an independent, clinically credible view of what's actually happening, and the governance to act on it.

1Independent clinical and quality audits across residential and home care settings
2Root-cause review of incidents, complaints and regulatory findings
3Clinical governance framework design and uplift
4Policy, procedure and documentation review
5Board and executive reporting on quality and risk
1Review of current care plans against resident need and regulatory expectation
2Rebuild of care planning frameworks, templates and workflows
3Training for clinical staff on assessment and documentation
4Ongoing audit and support to keep standards embedded

02 — Care Planning Support

Care planning that actually reflects the person.

Care plans are often the first thing a regulator, funder or family looks at — and the first place gaps show up. We review it, rebuild it, and strengthen the clinical capability required to keep it working.

04 — Workforce & Capability

The right people, the right capability, where they're needed.

Sometimes the fastest way to change what's happening in care is to put experienced people into the service — clinicians, operators and specialists who've done this before.

1Rapid deployment of clinical, operational and specialist workforce
2Interim leadership for services in transition
3Tailored training and capability-building programs
4Workforce planning and role design
5Mentoring and on-the-ground coaching for existing teams
05 — Funding & Sustainability

Make sure funding reflects the care being delivered.

Funding models in aged care are complex and change often. We help providers analyse the opportunity, undertake clinical review, and translate legitimate opportunities into sustainable outcomes.

AN-ACC Funding Review Care Minutes Analysis Funding Documentation Audit Reassessment Support Revenue Reconciliation Sustainability Planning
1Mock audits and readiness assessments ahead of regulatory review
2Gap analysis against current standards and requirements
3Action plan development and implementation support
4Ongoing assurance monitoring once the change is in place

06 — Assurance & Readiness

Know where you stand before someone else tells you.

Audits, reviews and regulatory change are rarely a surprise — but being ready for them often is. We independently assess readiness, identify gaps, and help implement what needs to change before the review starts.
